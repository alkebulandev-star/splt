How it works

Three steps, in the order you already work.

1

Put Split Tracker on the master

It names the song and starts the session clock the moment you press play.

2

Drop Split Tag on each track

Tap the person who is on that track. Every tag links to the master by itself.

3

Agree the shares and lock

Shares must total 100% and everyone needs a legal name. Then one button seals it.

What it records

Everything a split dispute comes down to.

The plugin shows you a simple summary. Underneath, it keeps the full record so the summary can be backed up later.

SPLIT

Split sheet

Artist name and legal name, role, writer and master share, PRO, IPI and publisher for each person.

TAG

Who is on each track

Split Tag marks a track with a person, so the session knows whose vocal, beat or guitar it is.

TAKE

Numbered takes

Each recording pass is logged with who, when, how long and where in the song it started.

TIME

Session time per person

Time played and time recorded, per person and per track, across every session on the song.

STEM

Stem fingerprints

When you bounce, each tagged track gets its own audio fingerprint, filed under the right artist.

BOUNCE

Bounce receipt

One receipt per bounce, listing the master, its stems, the takes and the splits at that moment.

PROJECT

Project-file signature

Sign the Logic, FL Studio or any other project file, so you can show which version was yours.

LOG

Tamper-evident log

Every entry is chained to the one before it. Change one line and the chain no longer checks out.

IDENTIFY

Identify a track

Sign up, drop in any audio, and see which of your masters or stems it is. Your plugin fills the library for you.

Privacy

It keeps records. It does not listen.

No voice recognition

People are tagged by hand. Nothing tries to identify anyone from how they sound.

Your audio stays put

If you link an account, the plugin uploads your records and fingerprints. A fingerprint is a code worked out from the sound; the sound itself is never copied or sent.

Works without internet

No connection in the studio? The plugin saves one receipt file. Send it to the song's owner, who uploads it after signing in to register the song. It carries records and fingerprints, never audio.

Everything is disclosed

The privacy policy lists every item the plugin stores, including the detail the screen does not show.

Upload an offline receipt

For studios with no internet

The plugin normally sends its records to your account by itself. When the studio computer is offline, or is not linked to an account, the same records can travel as one file instead.

  1. Tag the tracks first. Split Tag goes on every track, with the artist who performed it chosen. Untagged tracks are not credited to anyone.
  2. In the plugin, open the Evidence tab and press Save offline receipt. It saves one file ending in .splitreceipt.
  3. Send the file to the song's owner by email, WhatsApp, AirDrop or a USB stick. If you are the owner, carry it to any computer that is online.
  4. The owner uploads it here and chooses what to do with it. You see everything in the file before anything is saved.

A receipt holds the split, the session records, the tamper-evident log and the fingerprints. It never holds audio. The website checks the file is whole and re-checks the log. Who made it is what the file says, so it is shown as "offline receipt" on the song. Once that computer is online and syncs by itself, the live record replaces the receipt.

Profile

Personal details and affiliations

Your artist name is what collaborators see. Your legal name goes on the split sheet.

Your Artist ID

Give this to anyone adding you to a split sheet. They type it into Split Tracker, or into "Add collaborator" here, and your name and details fill in and the split comes to this account for your approval. It is safe to share: it cannot sign in or upload anything.

Plugin link

A plugin key is different from your Artist ID: keep it private. It lets Split Tracker on one computer upload its records to this account: splits, session time, takes, bounce and stem fingerprints and its log. Never audio. Create one key per computer and paste it into the plugin's Evidence tab. Your keys stay listed here, so you can copy one again whenever you need it.
