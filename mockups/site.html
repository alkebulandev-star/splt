How it works

From the first session to a proof nobody can rewrite

1In the DAW

Tag the tracks

Drop Split Tag on each vocal or instrument track and pick who it belongs to. The master plugin sees every tag.

2While you work

Sessions get logged

Time open, playback, recording, and who was checked in. Every entry is chained to the one before it, so edits show.

3On every bounce

A receipt prints

Splits at that moment, the people on each track, and an audio fingerprint of the exact file you exported.

4In the cloud

Proof gets anchored

Only a hash leaves your computer. It is timestamped on a public blockchain, so the date can't be faked later.

What's inside

Built for how songs really get made

Producers hand projects to engineers, writers send demos on WhatsApp, and beats get reused. Split Tracker keeps the record straight through all of it.

In the plugin

Real split sheets

Writer and master shares, role, PRO, IPI/CAE, publisher. Both totals must hit 100% before the banner turns green.

In the plugin

Bounce reminders

Every offline export is caught. If splits aren't confirmed, a pop-up says so and the bounce is logged anyway.

In the plugin

Printable receipts

A receipt is saved after each bounce with signature lines, ready to print or keep as PDF.

In the plugin

Audio fingerprint

Matches a WhatsApp MP3 or a streaming rip back to your bounce, even after mastering. Tested at 88–100% on copies.

Artist portal

Sign off by phone

Each writer approves their own share with a one-time SMS code. The approval goes on the record.

Cloud

Theft watch

Released songs are scanned against your fingerprints. Matches are flagged to every writer on the split.

Evidence

If there's a dispute, the record speaks

Each log entry stores a SHA-256 hash of the entry before it. Delete or change one line and the chain breaks at that exact spot. The daily summary hash is anchored to Bitcoin through OpenTimestamps, so the date is public and permanent.

Names, splits and IPI numbers never go on the chain. Only the hash does.

Your data

What we record, in plain words

What you see in the plugin and portal

  • Splits, roles and registration numbers
  • Session time and who was checked in
  • Bounces, receipts and approvals

What we also keep, for evidence

  • The full tamper-evident log of every change
  • Which computer opened the project, and when
  • Downloads of project files and receipts
  • Audio fingerprints of each bounce (not the audio)

All of this is listed in our privacy policy under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). You can ask for a copy of everything we hold about you.

No IPI number yet?

Writers without a registration get flagged in the plugin. Our registration service sets you up with GHAMRO and gets your IPI, so your share can be paid.
